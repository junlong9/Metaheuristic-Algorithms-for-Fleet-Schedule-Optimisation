Why Maintenance Scheduling Matters
---
In 1995, a US Army Blackhawk battalion reported 89% readiness. But when ordered to deploy, the commander mandated that no aircraft could deploy if it had less than 75 hours remaining until its next phase maintenance (Pippin, 1998).
9 of 24 helicopters were immediately non-deployable due to poor maintenance staggering.
This represented a massive loss of power.
---
