Priority Based Selection Hyperparameter Analysis Across Three Forecasts
Original moderate stagger and wide stagger datasets
David LongUniversity of Queensland and Northrop Grumman14 October 2026
Main finding
The strongest broadly applicable setting for this maintenance-only benchmark is C30, Wide dense ladder. It reaches the highest measured worst-day availability on all three datasets. Its largest smoothness penalty relative to a separately tuned dataset winner is 0.56 percent. Dataset-specific settings remain preferable when the final smoothness improvement matters.

The study crosses 58 neighbourhood and selection settings with the actual original, moderate stagger, and wide stagger workbooks, producing 174 matched sweep cases. It also records repeat timings, reference checks, and any finalist budget extensions. All 203 run records use the same scoring dates and preserve maintenance opening states.
The moderate and wide datasets change initial maintenance ages and the required check mix. They are distinct problem instances. Their higher input availability is not an optimisation gain on the original forecast. All reported gains compare a configuration with the default on the same dataset.
The previous half-step recommendation reaches 19 available aircraft on wide stagger, compared with 20 for the default. It therefore loses on the primary objective on that dataset and should not be adopted universally from the original-only result.
This report extends the original-only study and replaces its recommendation as evidence for selecting settings across these three forecasts. It evaluates maintenance feasibility; operational constraints are audited separately.

The moderate input has three cohorts of eight aircraft with maintenance timelines advanced by one, three, or five model years. The wide input has six cohorts of four, advanced by one through six model years. A model year is 365 days. Cohort assignment uses the saved generation seed 42 and was not chosen by the optimiser.
The aged fleets can start at A, B, or C within the repeating A B A C A B A D sequence. The existing opening-state adapter retains each aircraft's saved sequence index and A, B, C, and D due clocks. Every check that started before the benchmark start of 21 January 2026 is frozen. The original dataset retains its production rebuild assumption that its opening A cannot precede the first due date.
Changing only FILE_PATH would incorrectly reconstruct the aged fleets as if all aircraft were at the original opening A position. The study instead reads .scratch/initial-maintenance-stagger/schedule-data.json and replays the correct opening state. Check counts, types, durations, and these opening clocks remain fixed within each dataset.
Unchanged A B C D rules respectively use durations of 14 28 84 126 days, intervals of 365 730 1460 2920 days, and early allowances of 42 70 70 70 days. These are domain inputs, not search hyperparameters.
All datasets have 24 aircraft, but their D counts and downtime differ. Mean availability should therefore not be compared across datasets as a pure measure of optimiser quality.

Matched dates objective and validation
All three inputs are scored from 2026-01-21 through 2038-01-18, inclusive, for 4381 daily observations. The internal coordinate origin is 2025-11-16, allowing checks that began before the scoring dates to retain their full intervals. The model horizon is day 4546 and includes the full ends of supplied checks crossing the forecast boundary.
The objective is the lexicographic tuple (minimum availability, negative population standard deviation, mean availability). A higher worst day outranks every smoothness gain. Scores are calculated with the same NumPy functions used by the production method, on the matched visible window. The algorithm never accepts a worse endpoint.
The earlier report used 4481 observations through 28 April 2038. The matched study uses the published 4381-day forecast through 18 January 2038. Checks crossing either boundary remain in the model. Their starts can change the maintenance counted inside the visible window, so the visible mean can change even though full check durations are conserved. Standard deviations from the two horizons must not be compared directly.
Verification of the study driver
All settings preserve legal starts, no overlap, the maintenance sequence, and due resets. Each endpoint is checked by complete rule replay. Daily availability is independently recomputed by the delta-array method and by aircraft occupancy masks. Incremental availability must match both exactly. Past checks, opening states, check counts, and durations are preserved, and every accepted score strictly improves.
Priority groups are cached within an iteration, unchanged maintenance intervals are omitted from availability updates, and clearly dominated proposals are screened conservatively. Retained proposals still receive the original exact score. Cached and reference neighbour lists match for every initial configuration and every final neighbourhood. Reference original-input runs also match complete trajectories and final schedule hashes after these performance changes.
The live source was reorganised under vn_priority_based_selection.py during execution. The sweep processes had already loaded the preceding version. Serial repetitions therefore load byte-identical frozen algorithm and adapter snapshots whose hashes match all sweep records. This prevents a source-version change from being mixed into the timing or quality comparison.
The 58-case sweeps run as independent processes. Their elapsed times are retained as audit observations but are excluded from performance comparisons. Finalist timing runs execute serially after all sweeps finish.

Half steps adds intermediate offsets inside each level. Dense pairs adds separate intermediate levels. These differ because the search restarts at its first level after every accepted move and takes the first neighbourhood containing an improvement. A union of offsets, a layered ladder, and a reversed ladder can follow different trajectories even when they use similar magnitudes.
The ablations restore one half-step level to its original pair while retaining the other levels. Local refinements add two-day finishing, forty-two-day initial movement, or twenty-one-day offsets within the first level. Their effects are conditional on the remaining ladder and should not be added together as independent contributions.
Complete ordered shift tuples for every setting are provided in the final catalogue. Some structures occur with more than one selection policy.

Before the first forecast D check, smaller checks do not reset its opening D clock. With a 126-day duration and 70-day early allowance, every legal placement covers the 56-day interval beginning at that D due date. The peak overlap of these forced intervals bounds the worst day at 18 for the original and 21 for each stagger input. A measured result matching its bound proves optimality of the primary objective under those assumptions; otherwise the remaining gap is unresolved.
These bounds do not establish globally optimal smoothness. The study is a broad discrete sweep on three related forecasts, including two synthetic maintenance-age variants, and uses each saved maintenance opening state. It does not establish performance on an independent real fleet, changed maintenance rules, other horizon lengths, or operationally constrained neighbourhoods.
The study retains each supplied check list rather than generating additional check cycles when rebuilds change dates near the forecast boundary. This matches the fixed-list baseline. A planning implementation that regenerates the forecast and adds newly required checks would need a separate verification of the resulting schedules and parameter ranking.

After every sweep finishes, run finalise_three_datasets.py to obtain serial finalist timings and cap extensions, including the shared setting. The script resumes only against matching input, opening-state, frozen algorithm, frozen adapter, and configuration records.
The per-dataset JSON files store scores, traces, audits, proposal counts, source hashes, and endpoint schedule hashes. Complete schedule JSON files are retained under three-dataset-schedules. The input workbooks, saved opening state, production optimiser, and operational configuration are not edited by the study.
