4 · Milestone 1
SIMPLE DATASET

Proved the optimisation objective

Shift maintenance events, reject overlaps, and rank schedules lexicographically.

Runtime: benchmark pending
5 · Milestone 2
FULL NEIGHBOURHOOD

Complete search improved quality—but did not scale

Every aircraft, event, and shift produced a candidate schedule.

Runtime pressure increased with dataset complexity
6 · Milestone 3
200 RANDOM SAMPLES

Sampling reduced work, but introduced uncertainty

Evaluate a limited random subset instead of every neighbour.

Faster, but non-deterministic candidate selection
7 · Milestone 4
PRIORITY-BASED SELECTION

Search where availability is worst first

Rank events, rebuild only the affected aircraft, and update availability incrementally.

Deterministic and substantially faster
